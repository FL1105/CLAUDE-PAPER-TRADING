# First real-data results (2026-10-06)

Data: SII-WANGZJ/Polymarket_data, 2026-07-06 to 2026-10-05. 10,000 wallets (hash sample of the
62,839 that pass the activity filters and have at most 1,000 trades), taker side, 2.68M trades,
279k markets (95% resolved). Config: `config/backtest_90d.yaml` (42-day trailing window,
3 epochs of 14 days). Not the forward-run config.

## Walk-forward wallet selection
| epoch | eligible wallets | share of p < 0.05 (null expects 5%) | mean z | selected (BH, q = 0.10) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-08-17 | 275 | 5.8% | +0.09 | 0 |
| 2026-08-31 | 248 | 3.2% | 0.00 | 0 |
| 2026-09-14 | 228 | 3.8% | -0.03 | 0 |

- The p-values look like the null: no detectable wallet skill in this sample.
- The naive "top 20 by past PnL" list (what a copy-the-leaderboard approach would use) lost
  money out of sample: ROI -30%, pooled z -0.66, 37.5% of wallet-epochs positive.
- With nothing selected, the backtest placed no trades, so all outcome gates fail. Only the
  integrity gates pass. Tier: none.

## Price calibration of taker BUYs (resolved markets, before fees and spread)
Overall ROI +0.7% on 390k trades. Prices are close to calibrated in every bucket except
buys at or below 5 cents, which lost 39%. No bucket shows a stable, cost-surviving edge.

## What this does and does not show
- It does not prove no wallet has edge. It is one 90-day window, a 10k-wallet sample,
  taker side only, and short markets dominate (many wallets have few resolved markets in the
  trailing window, which limits power).
- It is evidence against "copy the top wallets by PnL" and against an easy price-bias edge.
- Next checks, cheapest first: repeat on other hash samples and the full 62,839 wallets; a
  longer window; maker side; a per-category split.
